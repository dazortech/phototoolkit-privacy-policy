Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Dazor Tech ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Photo Toolkit mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains how Photo Toolkit accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the application.

1. About Photo Toolkit

Photo Toolkit is a photo utility application that provides features such as photo compression, resizing, cropping, image adjustments, and background removal.

Photo processing features are designed to process images locally on the user's device. Photos selected by the user are not intentionally uploaded to Dazor Tech's servers for image processing.

2. Information We Access

Depending on the features you use, Photo Toolkit may request access to photos or media on your device.

This access is used to allow you to select images that you want to process using the application.

Photo Toolkit does not require you to create an account or provide personal profile information in order to use the application's photo processing features.

3. Photos and Media

When you select a photo, the application may access the selected image so that it can perform the requested operation, such as compression, resizing, cropping, adjustment, or background removal.

Photo processing is performed locally on your device. Dazor Tech does not intentionally upload the selected photos to its own servers for processing.

Processed images may be saved to your device when you choose to save them. You may also use the Android sharing functionality to share an image with another application.

4. Advertising

Photo Toolkit uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner advertisements and rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process certain information from the device or app interaction in order to provide, measure, and improve advertising services, subject to Google's applicable policies and user choices.

Information handled by advertising services may include information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location information, app interaction information, and other information necessary for advertising and fraud prevention, depending on applicable settings and regulations.

Dazor Tech does not use Photo Toolkit to intentionally collect personal information such as your name, phone number, contacts, or the contents of your photos for advertising purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

Photo Toolkit may use third-party software development kits (SDKs) and services to provide application functionality and advertising.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

In particular, Photo Toolkit uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising services.

Users should review Google's privacy documentation for additional information about how Google processes information in connection with its advertising services.

6. Local Storage

Photo Toolkit may store certain application settings and usage information locally on your device.

This local information may include application preferences, language settings, usage limits, and other settings required for the application's functionality.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to Dazor Tech's own servers.

7. Premium Features and Purchases

Photo Toolkit may offer optional Premium features through Google Play's in-app purchase system.

If you purchase Premium, the transaction is processed through Google Play. Dazor Tech does not directly receive or store your full payment card or banking information through the Photo Toolkit application.

The application may receive information necessary to determine whether a Premium entitlement has been purchased and activated.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Dazor Tech does not intentionally retain copies of photos selected for processing on its own servers because photo processing is designed to occur locally on the user's device.

Photos and processed images stored on the device remain under the user's control and can be deleted using the device's normal file or photo management features.

Local application data may be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling Photo Toolkit, subject to the behavior of the Android operating system and Google Play services.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Photo Toolkit.

Because photo processing is performed locally on the device, selected photos are not intentionally transmitted to Dazor Tech's own servers for processing.

Third-party services used by the application may have their own security and privacy practices.

10. Children's Privacy

Photo Toolkit is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

11. Your Choices

You may control the photos that Photo Toolkit can access by using the permissions and privacy controls provided by your Android device.

You may also stop using the application or uninstall it at any time.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, services, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy will be updated.
